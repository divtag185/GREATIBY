Founded by Ibrahim Shamac in 2016 and officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in 2019, Greatiby Limited was born from both ambition and vision. The name Greatiby itself has a story — it was first created when Ibrahim had just gained admission into the University of Benin. While completing his registration, he needed a unique email address, and "Greatiby" was born. From that day, he has lived up to the name, turning it into a brand that is now on its way to becoming a household name.

Before launching Greatiby, Ibrahim had already built a remarkable portfolio across multiple industries — from oil & gas to entertainment, real estate, automotive, and cryptocurrency. He is the Director at Shamac Petroleum Company Limited, a reputable oil and gas firm with deep roots in Nigeria, and also serves as a Director at Tetera Autos, one of the largest and most respected auto dealerships in Lagos, Nigeria.

Whether you're selling Bitcoin, USDT, or other popular digital currencies, Greatiby offers the best market rates and instant payments — every time. Our operations are powered by cutting-edge technology, strict compliance with industry standards, and a genuine passion for serving our clients.

Our Founder's Story

Okwilague Shamac Ibrahim was born on 23rd September 1987 and hails from Jattu-Uzaire, Etsako-West Local Government Area of Edo State, Nigeria. A positive-minded individual, he upholds honesty and integrity as core values — firmly believing it is better to lose money in business than to lose one's integrity.

Ibrahim completed his secondary education at Greater Tomorrow Secondary School, Benin City, earning both his West African Examination Council (WAEC) and National Examination Council (NECO) certificates in 2004. He went on to obtain a B.Ed. in Electrical/Electronics Technology Education from the University of Benin in 2008.

In 2009, he commenced his National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) program in Ogun State, serving as a Site Engineer at the Office of the Governor, Bureau of Electrical Engineering Services, Oke'Mosan, Abeokuta. Upon completing his service in 2010, Ibrahim traveled to the United Kingdom to pursue his passion for movies by earning a Master's degree in Digital Visual Effects — a program he completed with distinction.

His professional career began in September 2012 at the Information and Communication Technology Agency (ICTA), Edo State, where he worked in the Website Unit until June 2016. In this role, he contributed to the planning, design, and development of the state's official web platform, created visual content, managed website updates, and collaborated with his team on key strategic and technical decisions.

Entrepreneurship has been a part of Ibrahim's life from a young age, inspired by his parents' business ventures. Over time, he built expertise in cryptocurrency trading and other business operations. He once ventured into movie production, producing African Messiah — featuring stars such as Nadia Buhari, Darlington Achakpo ("Speed Darlington"), Temidayo Omoniyi ("Zlatan"), Nosa Afolabi ("Lasisi Elenu"), Charles Okocha, and others. However, after the project did not yield a financial return, he decided to exit the industry, recognizing its potential to cause significant financial setbacks. Since then, his creative work in film has been limited to producing light-hearted, family-centered content.

Today, Ibrahim is the founder and CEO of Greatiby Limited, a trusted digital asset exchange known for competitive rates, instant payouts, and reliability. He is also involved in other business ventures, including automotive sales and petroleum marketing.

A great philanthropist at heart, Ibrahim remains deeply committed to community development and empowering others. His work ethic, values, and vision continue to shape his journey as a respected figure in business and beyond.

FROM CULTURE SHAPER TO GREATIBY PARTNER

Zlatan Ibile × Greatiby

Omoniyi Temidayo Raphael, professionally known as Zlatan Ibile, is one of Nigeria's most influential contemporary artists, entrepreneurs and cultural figures. From shaping the sound and movement of African street-pop to building businesses beyond music, Zlatan's journey has always been driven by ambition, authenticity and the courage to move differently.

Today, that journey enters a new chapter as Zlatan officially joins Greatiby as a Partner.

Born on December 19, 1994, and raised in Ikorodu, Lagos, Zlatan began gaining national attention after winning the Airtel One Mic talent competition in 2014. His mainstream breakthrough followed with My Body featuring Olamide, before records such as Able God and the hugely influential Zanku (Leg Work) transformed him into one of the defining voices of a new generation.

The Zanku movement became much bigger than a song. It introduced a dance, language and energy that spread across Africa and the diaspora, establishing Zlatan not simply as an entertainer, but as a genuine culture shaper.

Since then, Zlatan has built an extensive catalogue of major records and collaborations with some of Africa's biggest artists, including Burna Boy, Davido, Wizkid and Olamide. His albums — Zanku (2019), Resan (2021), and Symbol of Hope (2025) — demonstrate an artist who has continued to evolve while remaining connected to the culture that shaped him.

His influence extends well beyond music.

As the Founder and CEO of Zanku Records and creator of streetwear brand ZTTW (Zero To The World), Zlatan has consistently demonstrated an entrepreneurial mindset. His transition into technology and digital finance through Greatiby represents another step in that evolution.

Zlatan Ibile × Greatiby

Zlatan's relationship with Greatiby goes beyond the traditional celebrity endorsement.

The partnership brings together two stories built around growth and possibility.

Together, Zlatan and Greatiby are connecting culture, technology and financial innovation.
