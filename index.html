How fast are swaps processed?

Simply deposit your crypto into your EasyPay wallet address. You'll receive Naira directly into your bank account. The entire process is automated and takes just seconds.

What fees are associated with swaps?

Swapping involves a fee of 0.5% of the transaction amount.

Are swaps reversible?

No, swaps are permanent and cannot be undone.

What tokens can I swap?

You can swap Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and a wide range of other supported tokens directly within the app.
